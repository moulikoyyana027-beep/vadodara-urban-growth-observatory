Methodology, sources and limitations

Analysis workflow

🗺️ 1. Study boundary

Prepare the Vadodara study area.

🛰️ 2. GHSL datasets

Load built-up surface and population layers.

📊 3. Zonal statistics

Summarize pixel values within the boundary.

📈 4. Growth analysis

Calculate absolute change and percentage growth.

🌐 5. Visualization

Produce maps, charts and temporal comparisons.

🔎 Interpretation of Results

Built-up surface: The yearly maps and time-series chart show how the spatial distribution of built-up surface changes across the selected GHSL epochs. Compare the numerical values and percentage changes to identify the periods with the largest increase.

Population: The population maps and chart show how the estimated population distribution changes across the study area. Differences between years can help identify areas for further investigation of service and infrastructure requirements.

Growth comparison: Compare built-up and population percentage growth using the same baseline year. If built-up growth exceeds population growth in the calculated results, this may indicate a need to examine land consumption and development density. It does not, by itself, prove a causal relationship.

Data limitation: The 2030 GHSL epoch is projected/modelled. Treat it separately from historical observations. Interpret all values according to the selected GHSL bands, units, resolution and study boundary.

Data sources and limitations. Global Human Settlement Layer (GHSL), European Commission Joint Research Centre. Dataset products have specific definitions, units, spatial resolutions and epoch characteristics. Pixel values and zonal summaries should be interpreted according to the selected GHSL bands and metadata. The 2030 epoch is projected/modelled and must not be described as a direct observation of future conditions.
